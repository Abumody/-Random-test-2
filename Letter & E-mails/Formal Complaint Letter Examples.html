Formal Complaint Letter Examples

Three complete examples for each section: Reasons for Writing, Main Body, and Conclusion

Reasons for Writing - Introduction Examples

The introduction of a formal complaint letter should immediately state the reason for writing. It should clearly identify what/who you are complaining about, when and where it happened, and why you are dissatisfied. This sets the context for the entire letter.

Airline Service Complaint

Dear Sir/Madam,

Introduction Paragraph

I am writing to express my deep dissatisfaction with the poor service I received when travelling with your airline on 30th January. I flew from London to New York on flight TMN108 and was completely disappointed with the overall experience.

Key Elements:

  • Clear statement of dissatisfaction
  • Specific date and flight details
  • Route information
  • Sets tone for the complaint
Airline Service Clear & Specific
Restaurant Experience

Dear Sir/Madam,

Introduction Paragraph

I am writing to formally complain about the unacceptable dining experience my family and I had at your restaurant, "Bella Vista", on Saturday, 15th November. Despite our high expectations based on your reputation, we were extremely disappointed with multiple aspects of our visit.

Key Elements:

  • Specific restaurant name and date
  • Mention of family group
  • Reference to expectations vs reality
  • Hints at multiple issues

Product Quality Issue

Dear Sir/Madam,

Introduction Paragraph

I am writing to bring to your attention a serious quality issue with the "Premium Pro Laptop" (model #LP-2023) that I purchased from your online store on 5th March. The product, which arrived on 10th March, has been defective since arrival and does not function as advertised.

Key Elements:

  • Specific product name and model
  • Purchase and delivery dates
  • Clear statement of defect
  • Reference to advertising claims
Product Complaint Technical Details

Introduction Writing Tips

Always include: specific dates, exact names (of products, services, or locations), and clear statement of dissatisfaction. Avoid emotional language - stick to facts. The introduction should make the reader immediately understand what the complaint is about.

Main Body - Complaint Details Examples

The main body contains your specific complaints, each in a separate paragraph. Use transition words like "Firstly", "Secondly", "Furthermore", "What is more" to connect paragraphs. Provide examples and evidence for each complaint. This is where you detail exactly what went wrong.

Airline Service - Main Body
Main Body Paragraphs

Firstly, the on-board service was extremely slow and careless. When I requested a glass of water, it took the flight attendant thirty minutes to bring one, and the glass provided was visibly dirty with fingerprints.

What is more, the flight attendants displayed consistently rude behavior. One attendant spilled hot coffee on my shirt and did not apologize or offer assistance to clean it. This conduct was the complete opposite of the "helpful and friendly" staff promised in your advertisements.

Furthermore, the in-flight meal was completely unsatisfactory. It was served cold and had an unpleasant taste, which was quite unlike the "delicious gourmet meal" you advertise in your promotional materials.

Structure Analysis:

  • Three distinct complaint paragraphs
  • Clear transition words
  • Specific examples for each point
  • References to advertising claims
3 Paragraphs Good Transitions
Restaurant - Main Body
Main Body Paragraphs

To begin with, we waited forty-five minutes for our starters despite the restaurant being only half full. When the food finally arrived, my soup was lukewarm and my wife's salad contained wilted lettuce.

Moreover, our main courses were incorrectly prepared. I specifically ordered the steak medium-rare, but it arrived well-done and tough. My daughter's pasta dish was excessively salty and inedible.

Additionally, the service was inattentive throughout the evening. Our water glasses remained empty for extended periods, and when we asked for the dessert menu, we waited twenty minutes before a server attended to us.

Structure Analysis:

  • Separate paragraphs for different issues
  • Specific food quality complaints
  • Service timing problems detailed
  • Affects multiple family members
3 Paragraphs Food & Service
Product - Main Body
Main Body Paragraphs

First of all, the laptop battery life is significantly shorter than advertised. Your website claims "up to 10 hours of battery life", but in practical use, it lasts no more than 3 hours with normal browsing and document work.

Secondly, the keyboard has several malfunctioning keys. The "E", "R", and spacebar keys frequently fail to register presses, making typing extremely frustrating and time-consuming.

Furthermore, the laptop overheats excessively during normal operation, reaching temperatures that make it uncomfortable to use on my lap. This also causes the system to slow down dramatically and occasionally shut down without warning.

Structure Analysis:

  • Technical issues clearly explained
  • Comparison to advertised specifications
  • Impact on usability described
  • Specific components identified
3 Paragraphs Technical Details

Main Body Writing Tips

Use one paragraph per main complaint. Start each paragraph with a transition word (Firstly, Secondly, Furthermore, Moreover, In addition). Be specific with examples - mention times, names, exact problems. Explain how each issue affected you. Compare reality to what was promised/advertised.

Conclusion - Expected Action Examples

The conclusion states what action you expect to be taken to resolve the complaint. Be specific about what you want (refund, replacement, apology, etc.) and set a reasonable deadline. Provide your contact information and thank them for their attention to the matter.

Airline Service - Conclusion
Conclusion Paragraph

I am extremely disappointed with this experience. I expect a full refund for the cost of my flight ticket, as the service received did not meet the standards advertised by your airline. Additionally, I would appreciate a formal apology for the appalling service. I look forward to hearing from you regarding this matter within the next 14 days. Please contact me at the address or phone number provided above.

Key Elements:

  • Clear statement of disappointment
  • Specific demand: full refund
  • Request for formal apology
  • 14-day deadline for response
  • Contact reference
Refund Request 14-Day Deadline
Restaurant - Conclusion
Conclusion Paragraph

This dining experience was far below the standard we expected from an establishment of your reputation. I request a full refund of our bill amounting to £145.80, as well as a complimentary voucher for a future visit to give your restaurant an opportunity to demonstrate the quality service you advertise. Please respond to this complaint within 10 days. I can be reached at the email address or phone number provided above.

Key Elements:

  • Specific refund amount mentioned
  • Additional request: complimentary voucher
  • 10-day response deadline
  • Opportunity for restaurant to improve
  • Contact information reference
Specific Amount Voucher Request
Product - Conclusion
Conclusion Paragraph

Given the multiple defects in this product, it is clearly not fit for purpose as advertised. I request either a full refund of £899.99 or a replacement with a properly functioning unit. If you choose to provide a replacement, I expect it to be shipped within 7 days of receiving this complaint. Please contact me within 5 business days to confirm how you will resolve this issue. I can be reached at the contact details provided above.

Key Elements:

  • Two options: refund OR replacement
  • Exact refund amount specified
  • 7-day shipping deadline for replacement
  • 5-business-day response time
  • "Not fit for purpose" legal term used
Refund or Replace 7-Day Shipping

Conclusion Writing Tips

Be specific about what you want: exact refund amount, replacement, apology, etc. Set a reasonable deadline for response (typically 7-14 days). Provide your contact information (though this is usually in letterhead). Maintain a firm but polite tone. End with a standard closing like "I look forward to hearing from you" or "I hope to resolve this matter promptly."

Complete Complaint Letter Example

This is a complete formal complaint letter combining all three sections. Notice how it flows from introduction to main body to conclusion, maintaining a formal tone throughout while clearly stating the problem and expected resolution.

Complete Airline Complaint Letter

Dear Sir/Madam,

INTRODUCTION

I am writing to complain about the poor service I received when I travelled with your airline on 30th January. I flew from London to New York on flight TMN108 and I was completely dissatisfied.

MAIN BODY - Paragraph 1

Firstly, the on-board service was very slow and careless. When I asked for a glass of water it took the flight attendant half an hour to bring one, and the glass which she brought was dirty.

MAIN BODY - Paragraph 2

What is more, the flight attendants were all very rude. One split a coffee on me and didn't apologise or help to clean it up. They were the complete opposite of the "helpful and friendly" staff you describe in your advertisement.

MAIN BODY - Paragraph 3

Furthermore, the food was awful. It was cold and tasted horrible. Again, this was quite unlike the "delicious in-flight meal" you advertised.

CONCLUSION

I am very disappointed indeed. I would like a full refund for the cost of my flight as well as an apology for the appalling service. I hope to hear from you regarding this matter as soon as possible.

Yours faithfully,

Ken Hampton

Complete Letter Tips

A complete formal complaint letter should flow logically from problem statement to specific complaints to desired resolution. Maintain consistent formal tone throughout. Use proper salutations and closings. Keep paragraphs focused - one main idea per paragraph. Proofread for spelling and grammar errors before sending.
