Application Letter Paragraph Examples

Learn how to write each part of an application letter for different jobs. Use these examples as models for your own letters.

How to Use This Page:

1. Click on a job title to see examples for that job

2. Look at the 5 different parts of an application letter

3. Each part has examples for all 4 jobs

4. Use the examples to write your own application letter

1. Reason for Writing (First Paragraph)

Start your letter by saying what job you want and where you saw it.

Graphic Designer
I am writing to apply for the graphic designer position that was advertised on your company website. I saw the job listing last week and I was very excited to apply.
Secretary
I am writing to apply for the secretary position which was advertised in the local newspaper. I saw the advertisement on Monday and I am very interested in this job.
English Teacher
I am writing to apply for the English teacher position at your school. I saw the job advertisement on the school website and I would like to apply for this position.
Healthcare Worker
I am writing to apply for the healthcare worker position at City Hospital. I saw the job advertisement in this month's hospital newsletter.

2. Profile and Qualification

Talk about your education, studies, and qualifications.

Graphic Designer
I am a design student in my final year at City College. I am studying Graphic Design and I have learned how to use Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, and InDesign. I also have certificates in digital design and web graphics.
Secretary
I have a diploma in Office Administration from Business College. I studied typing, computer skills, and office management. I can type 60 words per minute and I am good with Microsoft Office programs.
English Teacher
I have a university degree in English Literature. I also have a teaching certificate from Teacher Training College. I speak English fluently and I have studied English grammar and teaching methods.
Healthcare Worker
I have completed a healthcare assistant course at Medical Training Center. I have certificates in first aid, patient care, and health safety. I am trained to help patients with daily activities and basic medical care.

3. Past Experience

Talk about jobs you have done before and what you learned.

Graphic Designer
Last summer, I worked as a design assistant at a small advertising company. My duties included helping with logo designs, creating social media graphics, and preparing presentation materials. I learned how to work with clients and meet deadlines.
Secretary
For the past two years, I have worked as an office assistant at Smith & Company. My duties included answering phones, organizing files, making appointments, and writing emails. I learned how to manage an office and help visitors.
English Teacher
I have worked as a teaching assistant at Greenhill School for one year. I helped students with reading and writing exercises, prepared classroom materials, and supported the main teacher during lessons. I gained experience working with children of different ages.
Healthcare Worker
I have volunteered at Sunshine Care Home for six months. I helped elderly residents with meals, personal care, and daily activities. I also assisted nurses with simple medical tasks and kept patient records updated. This experience taught me how to care for people with patience and kindness.

4. Personal Qualities

Say what kind of person you are and why you are good for the job.

Graphic Designer
I am a creative person with good attention to detail. I enjoy working on design projects and I always try to create original and beautiful work. I am also good at working in a team and accepting feedback to improve my designs.
Secretary
I am an organized and reliable person. I am always on time and I pay attention to small details. I enjoy helping people and I am good at solving problems in the office. I work well both alone and as part of a team.
English Teacher
I am a patient and enthusiastic person who enjoys helping students learn. I am good at explaining difficult ideas in simple ways. I believe every student can learn and I enjoy creating interesting lessons that help students understand English better.
Healthcare Worker
I am a caring and compassionate person. I enjoy helping people who need care and support. I am patient with people who are sick or elderly, and I always treat everyone with respect and kindness. I work well under pressure and I am good at following instructions.

5. Closing Remark (Last Paragraph)

End your letter politely and say you want an interview.

Graphic Designer
I enclose my portfolio and CV. I would be very happy to attend an interview and show you more of my design work. I am available to meet at any time that is convenient for you.
Secretary
I enclose my CV and references. I would be glad to attend an interview and discuss how I can help your office. I am available to meet next week at any time.
English Teacher
I enclose my CV and teaching certificates. I would be very interested in attending an interview and discussing how I can help your students learn English. I am available to meet after school hours or on weekends.
Healthcare Worker
I enclose my CV and training certificates. I would be very glad to attend an interview and talk more about my experience in healthcare. I am available to meet at any time that works for you.
